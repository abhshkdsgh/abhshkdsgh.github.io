Nephrotic Diary Online

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 8, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Nephrotic Diary Online. We are committed to protecting your privacy and your sensitive medical data. This app is designed to help patients manage Nephrotic Syndrome by tracking urine protein levels and medications.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information

We collect information that identifies you, including your name, email address, and contact number during account registration via Firebase Authentication.

Health Information (Sensitive Data)

The app collects and stores health-related data, including:

  • Urine protein levels (Dipstick results).
  • Current medication names, dosages, and frequencies.
  • Other medical issues or symptoms reported by you.
  • Remarks or notes regarding your health status.

3. Data Security & Encryption

Zero-Knowledge Architecture: Your health data is encrypted locally on your device using a Master Key known only to you. This means your medical records are encrypted before they are sent to our servers.

We utilize Industry-standard AES encryption. Neither the app developers nor Google (our cloud provider) can read your health records. If you lose your Master Key and Recovery Key, we cannot recover your data for you.

4. Sharing Data with Doctors

You may choose to link your diary with a healthcare professional by providing their unique Doctor Code. When you do this:

  • Your diary's symmetric key is encrypted using the doctor's public key.
  • The doctor can then view your records through their authorized "Doctor" version of the app.
  • You maintain full control and can unlink your doctor at any time, revoking their future access.

5. Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase for the following infrastructure:

  • Authentication: To manage user accounts securely.
  • Cloud Firestore: To store your encrypted records.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all data stored in your account.
  • Edit or correct your records.
  • Delete your account: Deleting your account will permanently remove all personal and health data from our servers.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 1, 2026 | Target Region: India

Welcome to DM-Neonat_AIIMS-Nagpur ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our mobile application, web application, and related services (collectively, the "App").

Geographic Scope & Privacy Commitment: This Privacy Policy applies strictly to users located within the Republic of India, complying with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA). Furthermore, DM-Neonat_AIIMS-Nagpur utilizes a Zero-Knowledge Encryption Architecture ensuring that your sensitive academic data is encrypted client-side and cannot be read or accessed by us or third parties.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide, maintain, and secure our academic tracking and program management services:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and authentication credentials provided during sign-up/login via Firebase Authentication.
  • Academic & Program Data: Information regarding programs, courses, activities, experience logs, assignments, resources, and gradebook records created or tracked within the App.
  • Device & Usage Data: Technical logs, app crash data (via Crashlytics), and performance diagnostics to ensure app stability.
  • Encryption & Security Data: Client-side encryption keys, cryptographic mnemonics (managed securely via local storage and encryption managers) to protect your sensitive academic records.

2. Purpose of Processing Personal Data

In accordance with the DPDPA, 2023, we process your personal data based on your explicit consent and for lawful purposes related to providing the DM-Neonat_AIIMS-Nagpur service:

  • Authenticating user identity and managing user accounts (Trainees and Teachers).
  • Tracking academic progress, logging experience, managing programs, and syncing data securely across your authorized devices.
  • Ensuring data security through end-to-end encryption mechanisms.
  • Providing customer support and responding to inquiries or grievances.

3. Data Storage, Security & Zero-Knowledge Encryption

Your data is stored securely using cloud infrastructure (Firebase/Google Cloud) combined with an uncompromising Zero-Knowledge Encryption Architecture:

  • Client-Side Key Generation: Cryptographic keys (such as RSA identity keys and AES program keys) and recovery mnemonics are generated locally on your device. We never generate or store your plaintext keys on our servers.
  • Zero-Knowledge Guarantee: All sensitive academic records, experience logs, and program data are encrypted on your device before being synced to the cloud. Because decryption keys remain solely in your possession (secured by your device and cryptographic mnemonic), we (the service providers) cannot read, view, or decrypt your personal academic data.
  • Security Measures: We implement robust technical safeguards to protect metadata and encrypted data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.
  • Retention Period: We retain your data only as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. You may request account deletion at any time.

4. Your Rights as a Data Principal

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA), you (as a Data Principal) have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: You have the right to request a summary of your personal data processed by us and the processing activities.
  • Right to Correction and Erasure: You can request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data, or erasure of your personal data when it is no longer necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: You have the right to register a grievance with our Grievance Officer regarding the processing of your personal data.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw your consent to data processing at any time by deleting your account or contacting us. Note that withdrawal of consent may affect your ability to use the App.

5. Children's Data

DM-Neonat_AIIMS-Nagpur is intended for academic and educational tracking purposes. If users are under 18 years of age (minors under Indian law), verifiable parental/guardian consent is required in accordance with the DPDPA before providing personal data.

6. Third-Party Services

We utilize trusted third-party services (such as Firebase by Google) for authentication, cloud database, and crash reporting. These services adhere to stringent data protection standards and process data in accordance with applicable laws.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements under Indian law. We will notify you of any significant updates through the App or via email.
